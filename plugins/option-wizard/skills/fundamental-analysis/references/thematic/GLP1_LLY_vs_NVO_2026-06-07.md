# GLP-1 Market Share & Pipeline Showdown — LLY vs NVO

**Date:** 2026-06-07
**Context:** Follow-up to NVO single-name deep dive (2026-06-06). User asked for a structured comparative thesis on whether Lilly will completely override Novo's GLP-1 market share, with explicit consideration of Alzheimer's/oncology adjacencies and a catalyst-driven share-price outlook.

---

## Core judgment (TL;DR)

1. **LLY will NOT completely override NVO** — but will become the long-term 60-65% share dominant player. NVO retreats to 30-35% second-tier (not eliminated, but loses premium pricing power).
2. **The "GLP-1 for everything" thesis has been TRUNCATED** by the Evoke Alzheimer's failure (late 2025 topline, full Lancet results 2026).
3. **Oncology is REAL-WORLD noise** — strong epidemiological signal (38-50% reduction in metastatic progression for breast/CRC/lung/liver, 34% mortality reduction at ASCO 2026), but no RCT label exists. Not estimable in current valuations.
4. **Next definitive catalyst = LLY retatrutide TRIUMPH-3 (CV outcome) in H2 2026** — could redefine the entire GLP-1 industry hierarchy.
5. **NVO at 11.8x TTM PE still has upside**, but it requires a 18-24M wait for amycretin Phase 3 readout to refill the pipeline narrative.
6. **LLY at 34x PE is rich but has execution support** — pipeline execution risk < NVO's narrative vacuum risk.

- **Amycretin is NVO's last card but it's a 2028+ readout.** This is the entire option value embedded in NVO's current 11.8x PE.

## 6.5. Muscle preservation pipeline — the 5th structural gap for NVO

(Added 2026-06-07 follow-up: user question on GLP-1 variations preserving/increasing muscle %)

GLP-1 weight loss has a known severe side effect: **20-50% of total weight loss is lean (muscle) mass loss**. This has spawned a parallel "muscle-sparing add-on" pipeline that's near commercialization. Critically, **LLY has built bimagrumab in-house via the Versanis acquisition; NVO has no internal muscle preservation asset.**

### Pipeline matrix (by stage)

| Drug | Company | Mechanism | Stage | Key data |
|------|---------|-----------|-------|---------|
| **Bimagrumab + Sema** (BELIEVE) | **LLY** (Versanis subsidiary) | Anti-Activin Receptor IIB Ab | Phase 2b complete, **Phase 3 prep** | 22.1% weight loss / 72w, **92.8% fat loss**, some regions show net muscle GAIN; Lilly cancelled one Phase 2 (T2D indication) for "strategic reasons" → likely refocusing resources on retatrutide combo |
| **Trevogrumab + Sema** (COURAGE) | **REGN** | Anti-GDF8/Myostatin Ab | Phase 2b 26w interim readout EASD 2025 | Monotherapy preserves **50.8-51.3%** lean mass; **triplet (+garetosmab) preserves 80.9%** but tolerability issues, high discontinuation |
| **Apitegromab + Tirzepatide** (EMBRAZE) | **SRRK** | Selective Myostatin Ab | Phase 2 complete (positive) | + tirzepatide retains **54.9% more** lean mass (≈ +4.2 lbs) vs tirzepatide alone, 24w, well tolerated |
| **MariTide** (maridebart cafraglutide) | **AMGN** | GLP-1 agonist / GIPR antagonist (peptide-antibody conjugate, monthly) | Phase 3 underway (MARITIME-1/2/CV/HF/OSA/SWITCH) | Phase 2: 12-16% weight loss / 52w; monthly + different mechanism = adjacent track, NOT muscle-specific |
| **Enobosarm** (QUALITY, PLATEAU) | **VERU** | SARM (Selective Androgen Receptor Modulator) | Phase 2b ongoing | Targets **older sarcopenic obesity**; Wegovy combo Phase 2b enrolling, tirzepatide combo starts Q1 2026; **FDA has given regulatory clarity** |
| **SRK-439** | **SRRK** (next-gen) | 2nd-gen myostatin | Preclinical | Long-tail pipeline |

### Mechanism cheat sheet — 3 main pathways, each with tradeoffs

1. **Myostatin inhibition (GDF8 antibodies)** — Apitegromab / Trevogrumab. Most specific, lowest side effects, moderate efficacy (40-55% muscle preservation)
2. **Activin receptor inhibition (ActRII antibodies)** — Bimagrumab. Highest efficacy (can reverse lean mass loss, even net muscle gain), but activin pathway touches multiple tissues, long-term safety needs monitoring
3. **SARM (Androgen receptor modulation)** — Enobosarm. Directly stimulates anabolism, strong in elderly/sarcopenic populations, but endocrine side-effect concerns
